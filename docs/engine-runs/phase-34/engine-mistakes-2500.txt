What was done with them:

Eleven became mistakes. Eight of those were entries whose own note described losing the queen or a piece
while the grade said inaccuracy or playable: Qd5 onto the knight (6.h3 line), Nxc4 with the queen still on c7
(6.Ne5 line), Bg4 with nothing on f3 to pin so Qxg4 takes it (ch2 4.Bc4 and ch4 2.e5), Bf5 into the bishop on
d3 (6.Bd3 line), Nd4 into Nxd4 (exercise 3), ...Nc6 into Bxf7+ and Ne6 forking the queen (Tennison), and
...Be7 losing the bishop on g6 to h5 because its own pawn on h7 took its retreat (Blackmar-Diemer). The
other three (two ...Qd7 entries and 8...e5 in the 6.Ne5 line) were inaccuracies at 2.1 to 2.9 and are
mistakes now, with notes made true to the engine's line.

Thirteen playable entries between 0.94 and 1.6 became inaccuracies, with "fine", "sound" and "also good"
reworded to say what the move costs.

Four were too harsh. gxf6 (2.Nc3 line) at 0.52 is an inaccuracy, not a mistake. Tennison 3...e5 and 3...Bf5
are sound (the engine prefers e5 to the course's Nf6 by half a pawn) and are playable with their notes
corrected. And 6...Nxc3 in the Blackmar-Diemer is sound and its note was FALSE: the knight takes a knight
before the queen takes the bishop, and it gets away a pawn up (-0.50 for White). Rewritten as playable.
